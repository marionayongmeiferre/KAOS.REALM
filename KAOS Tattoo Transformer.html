STYLE PREVIEW

00 — Surreal Compose

Stitch multiple photos into a single impossible subject (cat-flower, head-tomato, etc.). Each element auto-cuts its background from the corners; drag to move, wheel to scale, shift+wheel to rotate. Pick Realistic or High Contrast finish — the merged result drops into the main canvas with levels auto-tuned.

Auto-tune

Surrealist auto picks black/white points at the 0.5%/99.5% histogram percentiles, sets gamma so post-level mean ≈ 118, and scales contrast by the source's standard deviation.

High-contrast auto additionally runs Otsu's threshold for the cut-off, measures lighting unevenness across a 12×12 tile grid, estimates high-frequency texture via neighbour differences, and computes Otsu separability for bimodality. Mode is picked: high separability + clean → Hard, uneven lighting → Adaptive, noisy mid-tones → Dotwork, line-art-like → Edges.

01 — Surrealist Photocollage

Desaturated photoreal subject, crushed shadows, preserved highlights, hard cut-out edges, heavy paper grain. Reads as a physical photo pasted to cream stock.

Recipe: grayscale → levels (BP, WP, gamma) → S-curve contrast → grain → multiply over cream paper. Background is removed via the Mask panel.

02 — High Contrast

Pure black on cream. Four modes:

Hard — global cut-off threshold. Fastest, cleanest for graphic subjects.
Dotwork — Pure blacks below the cut-off stay solid and crisp; the Stipple Opacity control sets how dark the transition band of dots is: low values keep the fringe airy and grey, high values push it toward solid black.
Adaptive — local-mean threshold. Each pixel compares against its neighborhood, so uneven lighting doesn't swallow the subject.
Edges — pure Sobel edge map. Outline-only rendering.

Pre-level normalizes the source (fixes dirty BP/WP, lifts midtones via gamma). Local boost is an unsharp pre-amp that exaggerates local contrast before threshold.

Outline lock / Edge overlay — overlays Sobel edges on top of any mode, so the main shape is guaranteed visible regardless of threshold.

Clean-up / Despeckle — removes tiny isolated ink specks (secondary reflections, sensor noise) and fills pinholes, then gently smooths the silhouettes. Essential for low-contrast metallic / reflective sources that otherwise read as "dirty". Auto-tune raises it when the figure isn't cleanly bimodal. Disabled in Dotwork (its dots are intentionally tiny).

03 — Halftone

Ben-day dot rasterization. Variable-radius black dots on a rotated grid. Recipe: sample mean luminance per cell → dot radius = (1 − L)^γ × boost × cell/2.

04 — Stipple / Engraving

Random-placed dot density imitating 19th-century engravings. Darker regions accumulate more dots. Reject samples where random() > local darkness.

Substrate

Cream / stone / bone paper, uniform grain, soft vignette, optional drop shadow under cutouts to sell the "pasted-on" effect.

Mask

Per-pixel keep/remove buffer. Auto from corners flood-fills from each corner within the tolerance radius. Click flood-fills wherever you tap. Erase/Restore brushes for cleanup.
